Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account to shorten a URL?

No. Paste a link and get a short URL immediately — no sign-up required.

Do short links expire?

No, short links don't expire on their own and keep working indefinitely.

How does click tracking work?

Each short link's click count is stored server-side and shown here for links created in this browser session. Clearing your browser data or switching devices won't show links from other sessions.
